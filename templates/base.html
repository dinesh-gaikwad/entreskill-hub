Platform Features

Everything needed for vocational job matching

The portal is designed for practical hiring in trade sectors like electrical, welding, fitting, machining, fabrication, mechanic work, and service-based technical roles.

01

Student Profiles

Benefits

Why this portal is useful for both sides

ITI graduates and diploma holders often depend on local contacts, notice boards, or informal groups to find work. This portal centralizes those opportunities so candidates can discover openings faster and employers can reach the right technical talent without extra effort.

For employers, the platform reduces manual screening by allowing them to filter candidates by trade skill, location, and education. For students, it improves visibility, application tracking, and long-term career planning in the vocational ecosystem.

FAQs

Common questions

Who can use this portal?

ITI students, diploma holders, apprentices, workshops, factories, manufacturing units, and admins can use the portal based on assigned roles.

What can students do?

Students can register, build profiles, search jobs, apply to openings, and track application status from their dashboard.

What can employers do?

Employers can register, create a workshop profile, post jobs, review applicants, shortlist candidates, and contact applicants.

Does the portal support mobile devices?

Yes, the layout is designed to be responsive so users can access it from phones, tablets, and desktops.
